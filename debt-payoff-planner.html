How it works

1. Add your debts
For each card or loan: what you owe, the interest rate and the minimum payment. It's all on your statement.
2. Say what you can pay
The total you can put toward debt each month. Drag the slider and watch your debt-free date move.
3. Follow the plan
It tells you which debt to attack first and what to pay on each one this month. When one is gone, its payment rolls into the next.

Nothing. We never receive your debts, balances or any usage data. The app makes no network requests of its own. If you email us, we use your email only to reply.
