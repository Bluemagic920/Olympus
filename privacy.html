ZEUS ONE OLYMPUS

Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026. Zeus One Olympus ("Olympus") is made by ProfitHarbor AI LLC, Phoenix, Arizona.

The short version

Olympus has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you put into it (passwords, codes, notes, documents, money data) is stored encrypted on your own PC. We never receive it and could not read it if we did.

What stays on your PC

All tool data is kept in the Zeus One folder in your Windows user profile, encrypted with AES-256 using a key derived from your password. Your Recovery Key is shown once, to you only.
