Zeus One Wi-Fi Inspector is a free Windows app that finds every phone, computer, TV, camera and gadget on your network, tells you what each one is, and warns you when a new one joins.

Free on the Microsoft Store

What it shows

Every device
Phones, laptops, smart TVs, doorbells, speakers, printers, plugs and consoles, with their names where they announce one.
Who made it
Apple, Samsung, Amazon, Roku, Ring... from the public IEEE registry built into the app.
Strangers
Mark your own devices once. Anything new gets a NEW badge, a warning and a Windows notification.
Wi-Fi safety
Checks your Wi-Fi's encryption (Open, WEP, WPA2, WPA3) and risky router settings like Telnet.

What it can't see

Wi-Fi Inspector can see which devices are connected, not what they're doing. How much data each device uses and which websites it visits pass only through your router; most routers show that in their own app or settings page. Newer phones use a random "private Wi-Fi address" that hides their maker; the app tells you when that's the case.
