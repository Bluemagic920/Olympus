Questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your PC, in the Zeus One folder in your Windows user profile, encrypted with AES-256. There's no Zeus One cloud and no account. Use Backup to keep encrypted copies on another drive.

Does it replace my antivirus?

Shield is built on Microsoft Defender, which is already part of Windows, and adds extra layers on top. If you use another antivirus, the rest of Olympus works alongside it.

What if I forget my password?

When you set up Vault (and the other encrypted tools) you get a Recovery Key. Keep it somewhere safe; it lets you set a new password.

Is Pro really a one-time payment?

Yes. You pay once through the Microsoft Store, and Pro is yours on every PC signed in to your Microsoft account. Every 2.x update is included.

Refunds?

Purchases are handled by the Microsoft Store, so refunds follow Microsoft's Store refund policy.

What does Olympus send over the internet?

Only what specific features need, never your passwords or files. The privacy policy lists every connection.

Is there an iPhone app?

Zeus One Authenticator for iPhone is free at auth.profitharborai.com, and Olympus sends codes to it securely.
