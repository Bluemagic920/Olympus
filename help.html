Getting started

What do I need to run Olympus?

A 64-bit PC with Windows 10 (version 1809 or newer) or Windows 11. Olympus uses Microsoft Edge WebView2 to draw its window. It is built into Windows 11. On an older Windows 10 PC, Olympus tells you if it's missing and links to Microsoft's free download.

Do I need an account?

No. Olympus has no sign-up and no account with us. The only account involved is your Microsoft account, which the Store uses for the download and the Pro purchase.

Where should I start?

Open Olympus and look at the Security Score on the Home screen. It checks your PC and lists the most useful thing to fix next. Each item opens the tool that fixes it.

Free & Pro

What's free and what's Pro?

Free forever: Shield, Updater, Phish Check, Password Generator, Breach Check, Photo Scrub, QR Forge and File Verifier. Olympus Pro unlocks the other tools: Vault, Authenticator, Sentinel, Privacy, Firewall, Identity, Secure Notes, Redact, PDF Forge, Clipboard Guard, File Locker, Backup, Shredder, Storm Map and Ledger.

How do I buy Pro?

Open any Pro tool in Olympus and choose Upgrade to Pro. The Microsoft Store checkout opens. Pro is a one-time payment, with no subscription.

I bought Pro but it still shows locked
  1. Make sure the Microsoft Store app is signed in with the same Microsoft account you bought with.
  2. In Olympus, open any Pro tool and choose I already bought it - restore.
  3. Still locked? Restart the PC and try Restore again. Microsoft's licence servers sometimes take a few minutes after a purchase.
Can I use Pro on my other PCs?

Yes. Install Olympus from the Store on any PC signed in to the same Microsoft account, open any Pro tool and choose I already bought it - restore.

Does Pro work offline?

Yes. Olympus remembers your Pro licence. It just needs to reach the Microsoft Store at least once every 45 days to confirm it.

How do refunds work?

Purchases are made and handled by Microsoft, so refund requests go through Microsoft. Sign in at account.microsoft.com/billing/orders, find the purchase and choose Request a refund. Microsoft makes the decision under its own refund policy.

Passwords & your data

I forgot my master password

On the unlock screen, choose Forgot password? Use Recovery Key and enter the Recovery Key Olympus showed you when you set up the tool. Then you can choose a new password. Without the password or the Recovery Key, nobody can open your data, including us. That's how the encryption keeps it safe.

I lost my Recovery Key

If you can still unlock with your password, make a new one: unlock the tool, open its settings and choose New Recovery Key. The old key stops working. Print it or keep it somewhere safe, away from the PC.

Where is my data kept?

In the Zeus One folder inside your Windows user folder (for example C:\Users\you\Zeus One). Everything sensitive in it is encrypted. Nothing is uploaded to us. See the privacy policy for the few features that use the internet and what they send.

Moving to a new PC
  1. On the old PC, open each tool that holds your data (Vault, Authenticator, Secure Notes, Identity, Ledger), open its settings and choose Export encrypted backup. Save the files to a USB drive or your OneDrive folder.
  2. Install Olympus from the Store on the new PC. If you have Pro, open any Pro tool and choose I already bought it - restore.
  3. Open each tool on the new PC, choose Restore from a backup file… and pick its file. Unlock it with the password it had on the old PC.
Can Olympus sync with my phone?

Olympus can send Authenticator codes to the free Zeus One Authenticator for iPhone at auth.profitharborai.com. Syncing the Vault between devices isn't available yet.

Tools

Is Shield a separate antivirus?

No. Shield is a control centre for Microsoft Defender, the antivirus already built into Windows. It runs scans, shows threats and turns protections on, all in one place. If you use another antivirus, Windows switches parts of Defender off and Shield will tell you so.

Sentinel shows alerts when Olympus is closed

That's Sentinel's background guard. It starts when you sign in to Windows so it can warn you about new devices on your Wi-Fi, new breaches and tripwire files being touched. To stop it, open Sentinel and turn off the main switch at the top.

Firewall or Privacy asks for permission

Changing Windows firewall rules and some privacy settings needs administrator rights, so Windows asks you to confirm. Olympus never changes these without you choosing to. Privacy changes can be undone from the same screen.

Phish Check says a link is dangerous. Is it sure?

Phish Check looks for the warning signs scammers use, such as lookalike names, brand-new domains, hidden redirects and pressure tactics. Treat Dangerous as "don't click", and contact the company through its official website or app instead.

Uninstalling

How do I uninstall Olympus?

Open Start, right-click Zeus One Olympus and choose Uninstall. Your encrypted data in the Zeus One folder is kept on purpose, so reinstalling picks up where you left off. To remove it too, delete that folder after uninstalling.
