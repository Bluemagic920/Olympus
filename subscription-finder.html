How it works

1. Download your statements
From your bank's and card's websites, as CSV (or Quicken OFX/QFX, or PDF). Twelve months catches yearly renewals too.
2. Drop them in
Subscription Finder spots every charge that repeats: weekly, monthly, yearly, even the ones hiding under one "Apple.com/bill" line.
3. Decide what stays
Mark what to keep and what to cancel. One click opens the company's own cancel page. Watch your yearly savings add up.

What it catches

Price rises
"Netflix went up from $15.49 to $17.99. That's $30 more a year."
Yearly renewals
A heads-up before Amazon Prime, antivirus or VPN renewals hit, while there's still time to cancel.
Forgotten trials
New charges that started recently, like a free trial that quietly turned paid.
Doubled-up services
Three streaming services, two delivery memberships, two music apps.
Still charging after you cancel
Add next month's statement and it tells you if something you canceled charged you again.
Bills too
Phone, internet, insurance and utilities, even when the amount changes each month.

Privacy policy

Effective November 23, 2026 · ProfitHarbor AI LLC ("we"), maker of Zeus One Subscription Finder ("the app").

The short version: your statements never leave your PC. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else.

WhenWhat happensWhere
You add a statementThe app reads the file you choose and keeps the transactions (date, description, amount) in one file encrypted with Windows' own data protection, so only your Windows account can read it. The app never asks for or uses your bank login.Only your PC
You click a "cancel page" or "how to cancel" buttonYour browser opens that company's website, or a DuckDuckGo search for how to cancel that service. The app doesn't send your data with it.That website
You save a calendar or spreadsheet fileThe file is saved where you choose on your PC.Your PC
Buying ProThe purchase is handled by the Microsoft Store. We never see your payment details.Microsoft

What we collect

Nothing. We never receive your statements, transactions, subscriptions or any usage data. If you email us, we use your email only to reply.

Deleting your data

Settings › Delete everything removes it all. Statements › Remove deletes one account's transactions. Uninstalling the app removes its data folder.

Children

The app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
