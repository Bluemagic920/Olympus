Privacy policy

Effective November 23, 2026 · ProfitHarbor AI LLC ("we"), maker of Zeus One Receipt & Warranty Keeper ("the app").

The short version: your receipts stay on your PC, encrypted. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else.

WhenWhat happensWhere
You add a receipt picture or PDFThe app copies the file into its own folder and encrypts it with AES-256. The key is protected by Windows' own data protection, so only your Windows account can open it. Small preview pictures are encrypted the same way.Only your PC
The app reads a receiptText is read from photos with the text recognition built into Windows, running on your PC, and from PDFs directly. The picture is never uploaded.Only your PC
You type item detailsNames, stores, prices, dates, serial numbers, the last 4 digits of a card if you add them, and notes are kept in one file encrypted with Windows' data protection.Only your PC
You open a receipt pictureA temporary decrypted copy is made so your picture viewer can show it, and it is deleted when you close the app.Your PC
You save a PDF, spreadsheet or calendar fileThe file is saved where you choose, not encrypted, so you can open or send it.Your PC

What we collect

Nothing. We never receive your receipts, purchases or any usage data. The app makes no network requests of its own. If you email us, we use your email only to reply.

Deleting your data

Settings › Delete everything removes every item and receipt picture. Uninstalling the app removes its data folder.

Children

The app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
